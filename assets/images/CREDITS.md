## What this means

* **CC0 / Public domain** — free to use commercially, no credit needed. Nothing to do.
* **CC BY-SA 4.0** — free to use commercially, **but** you must credit the author,
  name the licence, and link to it. Share-alike applies to modified versions of the
  *image*, not to your website as a whole.
* **UNRESOLVED** — the source file could not be traced back. Do not publish these
  until you either identify the source or replace the image.

## Recommendation

Replace all of these with photographs of your own installations, crew and vehicles.
That removes every attribution obligation, and real job photos convert far better
for a local trades business than generic stock.

If you keep any CC BY-SA image, add a credits line in the footer or on a dedicated
page, in this form:

> "Photo title" by Author, licensed under
> [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
